Game Rules and Tutorial

How to Play

Every player starts with a pre-selected number of dice and players take turns making bids over the number of dice with a certain value in the game. Each turn the player can either raise the current bid (or make a bid if no bid has been made) or call the previous player's bluff. If a bluff call is successful, the player who made the bid loses a dice and a new round begins. If it is unsuccessful and their bid turns out to be true, the current player who called the bluff loses a dice, and the game continues.

Winning the Game

You win the game by outlasting your opponents and being the last player in the game with any dice left.

Additional Tips

When playing pass and play with friends, make sure to do your best to trick your opponents into losing their dice against you. Bluffing, double bluffing or even triple bluffing can be an extremely effective way of coercing your opponents into making poor decisions. Remember: play the man, not the game.
